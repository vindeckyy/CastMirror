castcore.dll links FFmpeg (libavcodec, libswscale, libavutil) and libx264.
The FFmpeg build shipped here is configured with --enable-gpl and includes
x264, which is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or
later. As a result, castcore.dll and the package as a whole are distributed
under the terms of the GPL. You may copy and redistribute it under those terms.

Complete corresponding source code
  CastMirror:  https://github.com/vindeckyy/CastMirror  (the tag matching this version)
  FFmpeg:      https://ffmpeg.org/download.html
  x264:        https://code.videolan.org/videolan/x264
  MSYS2 package recipes used to build the DLLs in this folder:
               https://github.com/msys2/MINGW-packages
To request the source for a specific binary by mail or e-mail, open an issue at
https://github.com/vindeckyy/CastMirror/issues. We keep each release's source
available for at least three years.

CastMirror uses hardware H.264 encoders through Media Foundation when the GPU
offers one, and falls back to libx264 only when it does not.
